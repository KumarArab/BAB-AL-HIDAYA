About us

Clean buildings, working systems and honest service, since 2019.

Who we are

Your building’s long-term care partner

Bab Al Hidaya Building Maintenance & Cleaning Services L.L.C is a limited liability company licensed by the Department of Economy and Tourism, Dubai, to provide building maintenance and cleaning services.

We started with a small team of technicians and cleaners serving residential buildings in Al Qusais. Today we look after towers, offices, retail outlets, schools and villa communities across Dubai, Sharjah and Ajman.

We employ our own trained staff, run documented safety and quality systems, and hold the licences, approvals and insurance our clients need to work with us with confidence.
